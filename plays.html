Set Sophia up once. Then run any play whenever you need it.

Do Step 1 a single time — Sophia learns your business and remembers it for every play after, so you never answer the same questions twice. You're done when she says "You're set up ✓" and shows your Play Status Board. After that, a play is just one click.

① Set up once — Sophia learns your whole business

1
Export your profile

In your Suite, open Settings and click Export my setup. That one file holds your business, brand voice, prices, and tools.

2
Hand it to Sophia, once

Click the button below, press Send, then paste or attach that file. She reads it and asks only what's missing — one question at a time.

3
She saves it — you're set

Sophia stores your profile so every play already knows your business. She says "You're set up ✓" and shows which plays are live.

Not technical? Don’t fight it — grab Guided Setup ($199) in the Suite’s Integrations tab and we connect everything with you on a live screen-share. Most owners pick this.

② Run a play whenever you need it

Each play opens Sophia with the job pre-typed — just hit Send. She already knows your business from Step 1, shows you her work, and ends with a one-tap link to log it in your Suite. Nothing is sent, posted, or paid without your OK.
