FAQ

Questions we get.

Do I need to be technical?

No — and you don't do it alone. We set everything up with you on a call and make sure it's working before you rely on it. If you can use email, you're ready.

Where does my data live?

On your own computer. Sophia reads and writes to the apps you already use. Nothing goes to a JOOLT server unless you choose the hosted upgrade later.

What if I already use QuickBooks / HubSpot / etc?

Perfect. The Suite connects to what you already have — QuickBooks, HubSpot, Gmail, PayPal, Square, Twilio, Canva, Notion, DocuSign, and more. No migration required.

Can Sophia really answer my phone?

Yes — with the upgrade. She can pick up, take a message, answer common questions, and book appointments. You decide what she's allowed to do on her own.

What's the difference between the Suite and Sophia Presence / SMB Pro?

The Suite is your starter — we set it up with you so it's running from day one, and it lives on your machine with your free JOOLT Rep Agent helping in the background. Sophia Presence adds voice (phone, callbacks, real conversations) and is built with you — not self-serve, book a call. Sophia SMB Pro is full end-to-end automation with a dedicated human JOOLT partner — also book a call.

Why are Sophia Presence and SMB Pro book-a-call only?

Because they're tailored. Voice needs your number, your tone, and your call flow. Full automation needs to fit your workflow, not the other way around. We meet, we scope, we build. That's how we make sure it works.

Do I really get a free JOOLT rep? Is it a person?

Your JOOLT Rep is a helper built into your Suite — an AI teammate, not a person — which is why we can give it to every buyer for free. It keeps Sophia updated and lends a hand in the background. A real human is available on higher plans. More at joolt.io/agent-rep.

What if I want to resell this to my clients?

The Agency / Reseller plan is designed for consultants, agencies, and integrators. White-label the Suite, deploy for clients, share in Sophia upgrade revenue.
